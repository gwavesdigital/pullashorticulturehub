About Sri Satyadeva Nursery

A Vision Born in 1950,
Flourishing Through Generations

Founded in 1950 by visionary horticulturist Sri Pulla Venkata Rao in Kadiyapulanka, Andhra Pradesh, Sri Satyadeva Nursery has grown into one of the country's most renowned green destinations[cite: 10]. Over decades, under the leadership of his son, Sri Pulla Chinna Satyanarayana, the nursery expanded across multiple expansive units, serving nationwide government landscaping, botanical reserves, and home gardeners alike[cite: 10].

Today, Pulla's Horticulture Hub combines this generational plant wisdom with modern distribution, professional transit sheds, and tailored wholesale services[cite: 10].
